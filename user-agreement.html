User Agreement

Effective 26 October 2026

This agreement covers Deco, a wall-hanging planner that stores your work on the device you are using. By tapping One-Tap Login after you accept this agreement and the Privacy Policy, you enter a local session.

What Deco does

You create wall entries, photograph a wall with Take Photo or import one with Album, enter dimensions, add numbered frame outlines with sizes and hook offsets, set a centerline, review hook coordinates, pick a confirm date, preview a hanging sheet, then generate a PNG pack. Preview is free. Generating a pack uses Credits after you confirm. Voice can dictate notes after you confirm the words. Coordinates are calculated from the sizes you enter. Frame outlines on the wall photo are placement marks, not artwork uploads. Deco does not project AR, auto-level a wall, or sell artwork.

Local account

One-Tap Login creates a local session. There is no password and no Sign in with Apple. Log Out ends the session and keeps your walls. Delete Account removes the local account and the data it stored on this device.

Credits

Credits are consumable and stay on this device. A new install receives 8 local starter Credits. Those starter Credits are not an Apple product. Buying Peg1 through Peg8 uses Apple in-app purchase. One hanging-sheet pack costs one Credit after you confirm. Credits are not restored after you remove the app, clear its data, or delete the account. There is no Restore Purchases control. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Your content

Wall photos, notes, and generated sheets belong to you. They stay in the app sandbox unless you tap Save High-Res Image to Photos. Clear All Data permanently removes walls, media, example history, and remaining Credits on this device.

Acceptable use

Use Deco only with photos and notes you have the right to use. Do not use the app to create or store unlawful content.

Contact
