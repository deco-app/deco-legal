Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026

Deco is a wall-hanging planner that runs on the device you are using. This policy describes the local session, what the app stores on that device, and when it asks for hardware access. Deco does not upload your wall photos, hanging sheets, or notes to a Deco server.

Local sign-in

One-Tap Login opens a session stored only on this device. Deco keeps a device identifier and a session token in local app preferences. There is no password and no Sign in with Apple. That identifier is not sent to a Deco account server.

Log Out removes the session token. Wall entries stay on the device. Delete Account removes the local identifier, the session token, wall entries, photos, hanging sheets, and Credits from this install.

Data on this device

Wall names, dimensions, numbered frame outlines, frame sizes, materials, hook offsets, centerline heights, hook coordinates, confirm dates, wall photos, generated PNG hanging sheets, and Credit ledger records are stored in the app sandbox. Preferences such as metric units and the last centerline preset are stored in local app preferences. Removing the app, or using Clear All Data, deletes this local data.

Sample pictures shown before you add your own media are illustrations shipped with the app. They are not uploaded.

Camera

The camera is requested only after you tap Take Photo or Retake. The photo you accept is copied into the app sandbox for the wall entry you are planning. Deco does not scan the camera in the background.

Photo library

Read access is requested only after you tap Choose Photo or Album. Deco copies the item you select and does not change your library. Add-only access is requested only after you tap Save High-Res Image to Photos, and only to write those hanging-sheet images.

Microphone and speech

The microphone is requested when you tap Speak Notes. Speech recognition is requested at the same time. Dictation runs on the device. Recognized words are shown for review and are inserted into the wall name or notes field only after you confirm.

Purchases

Credit packs are consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. Deco keeps the resulting Credit balance on this device. The balance is not restored after you delete the app, erase its data, or delete the local account. Apple handles payment and refund requests. A refund or revocation reduces only the unused portion of that grant. Hanging sheets already generated stay on the device until you delete them or erase the studio.

Notifications

After you enter the studio, Deco may ask for notification permission with the system prompt. If you allow it, the device token is stored on this device. Deco does not upload your walls to a Deco server.

What we do not collect

There is no Deco analytics server receiving your wall photos. There is no cloud sync.
